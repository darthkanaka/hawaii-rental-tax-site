1. What we do, and what we don't

We prepare and file Hawaii rental property tax returns for our clients: the General Excise Tax (GET), the state Transient Accommodations Tax (TAT), and county Transient Accommodations Tax where it applies. We also help clients who are behind catch up on past periods.

A few things we are not:

  • We're not a CPA firm or a law firm. We're a tax return preparer registered with the Hawaii Department of Taxation as a Verified Practitioner. That registration lets us act for clients who authorize us. It isn't an endorsement by the Department, and it doesn't make us accountants or attorneys.
  • We don't prepare income tax returns, federal or state.
  • We don't give legal advice or represent you in audits, appeals, or court unless we agree to that separately in writing.
  • We don't manage your property, collect your rent, or decide how much tax to charge your guests. Those decisions are yours.

10. Our on-time guarantee

If a return we're responsible for is filed late or wrong because of our mistake, we pay the resulting penalty. Here's exactly what that means:

  • What's covered: penalties the Department of Taxation or your county assesses on a return we prepared, to the extent the penalty was caused by our error.
  • What's not covered:
  • The tax itself. You always owed it, so it stays yours to pay.
  • Penalties caused by records you sent late, records that were incomplete or inaccurate, a tax payment you didn't make, or anything else listed in Section 9.
  • Penalties for periods before we started working for you, unless they're part of a catch-up job we quoted.
  • Delays caused by a Department or county system outage or anything else outside our reasonable control, as long as we file promptly once we can.
  • How to claim:
  • Send us the penalty notice within 10 days of receiving it.
  • Let us respond to the Department first. Many penalties can be reduced or removed at no cost, and we'll try to get it abated before anything else.
  • We pay whatever penalty remains after that, directly or by reimbursing you.
  • Who's covered: clients whose account is active and paid up when the penalty period was filed.
  • If a penalty had more than one cause, we cover the part that traces to our error.

Your engagement letter repeats this guarantee for your specific properties.
