Questions

The things people actually ask

I got a letter from the state. What do I do?

Send it to us before you pay it. Most of these letters are estimates rather than findings, and an estimate can be wrong in your favour. We work out what's actually owed for those periods, file what's missing, and answer the notice. If there's a penalty worth challenging, we ask for it to be waived before anyone pays anything. More on letters here.

What does this cost?

Catch-up work is a flat fee from $500, quoted in writing before anything starts. One property with a couple of missed years is usually $500 to $750. Monthly filing is $49 to $59 per property. Property managers pay $39 per owner per month with a four owner minimum. Long-term landlords pay $249 a year per property. All of it on one page.

Are you a CPA?

No, and we're upfront about it. We're a tax return preparer registered with the Hawaii Department of Taxation as a Verified Practitioner, which is what lets us file your returns and deal with the Department for you. It's a registration, not an endorsement. We're not a CPA firm or a law firm, and we don't prepare income tax returns. Here's the full picture.

I live on the mainland and can't even log in. Can you help?

Yes, and this is one of the most common reasons people call. You don't need your own Hawaii Tax Online login for us to file for you. Once you sign Form N-848, the state power of attorney, we request access to your accounts and handle it from here. It works the same way if you're outside the United States.

Do you hold my tax money?

Never. When we file, the payment goes from your own bank account straight to the state. It doesn't pass through us, so there's no account of ours for it to sit in or go missing from. You see the amount before it leaves and you get the confirmation number afterwards.

Will you replace my accountant?

No. General excise tax and transient accommodations tax are separate state filings that most income tax preparers don't touch month to month, and that's the slice we handle. Your income taxes stay with whoever does them now, and at tax time we hand over clean records of everything we filed.
