Questions

The things owners ask most

Does a long-term rental owe Hawaii GET?

Yes. General excise tax applies to rent from long-term tenants too, at 4.5% of gross rent on every island. Stays of 180 days or more are exempt from TAT, but not from GET.

Doesn't Airbnb collect Hawaii taxes for me?

No. Airbnb and VRBO don't collect or file GET or TAT for Hawaii hosts. The owner registers, collects and files all of it, including bookings taken directly.

How often do I have to file?

It depends on the tax you owe in a year. Twice a year at $2,000 or less, quarterly at $4,000 or less, and monthly above that. GET and TAT are judged separately, so they can land on different schedules.

Is this tax advice?

No. It's general information based on the state's published rules. Your actual filing schedule is the one the Department of Taxation assigns you, and if your situation is complicated, a Hawaii CPA or enrolled agent can look at it.
