Behind on Hawaii rental taxes? Here's how you get caught up

I got a letter from the state or the county

Don't pay it yet. Letters look worse than they usually are, and your first move matters. The two that land most often:

  • A Rental Property Compliance Notice from the Department of Taxation. These come from reports that rent collectors have to file naming the owners they collect for. It means the state knows there's rental income attached to you and can't see returns against it.
  • A back-year assessment for county transient accommodations tax. On Oʻahu these come through the county's billing contractor and can cover several years at once. The number on the letter is an estimate, not a finding, and an estimate can be wrong in your favor.

Here's what you get: someone reads exactly what's being asked, you learn what's actually owed for those periods, the missing returns get filed, and the notice gets answered. If there's a penalty worth challenging, we ask for it to be waived before anyone pays anything.

I need a tax clearance to register or renew

This is the one with a deadline attached, so you're probably in a hurry. A tax clearance says the state and county have everything they're owed from you, and you can't get one while returns are missing.

  • Hawaiʻi Island, Bill 47. Short-term rental registration has been required since September 1, 2026, and it asks for your GET and TAT licenses and a clearance. The county's waiver of violation fees runs to December 31, 2026, so there's a real reason not to sit on it.
  • Oʻahu renewals. Registration renewal wants you current on GET, state TAT and county TAT.

What you get: everything outstanding filed, what's owed paid from your own bank account, then the clearance requested and sent to you. Give the deadline you're working to and you'll get an honest answer about whether it's reachable.

Nobody ever filed, and I've just figured that out

This one arrives three ways, and they all end up in the same place:

  • Your property manager was supposed to be filing and wasn't. Worth knowing: the returns are in your name, so the state comes to you, not to them.
  • You assumed Airbnb or VRBO handled it. Almost everywhere else they do. In Hawaii they don't, and they don't register you either. The obligation sat with you the whole time.
  • You live on the mainland and can't get into the system at all. Very common, and doubly so if you're not in the United States. We do this part for you.

Why waiting costs you more

Hawaii has no amnesty program for this. A bill to waive penalties and interest for owners converting to long-term rentals died in committee in 2024, and nothing has replaced it, so back liability stays owed in full. Late amounts grow at 5% a month up to 25%, plus interest, and transient accommodations tax unpaid more than 60 days picks up another 20%. The state also now gets reports from rent collectors naming the owners they collect for, so waiting to be found is not a plan. Coming forward yourself puts you in a much better position than being found, which is the part that doesn't show up on any rate table.

What to have ready

  1. Any letters from the Department of Taxation or your county, all pages.
  2. Your rental income records for the years in question. Airbnb and VRBO both let you download yearly earnings statements. Leases and direct booking records work too.

Questions people ask before they start

Can you get my Hawaii tax penalties reduced?

Sometimes. Hawaii can waive penalties for reasonable cause, and we ask whenever it is worth asking. We cannot promise an answer, and we never quote a job on the assumption that it will work. The tax itself and the interest stay owed either way.

How far back does Hawaii go on unfiled GET returns?

There is no statute of limitations on a return you never filed, so there is no year the state cannot ask about. Filing a year is what starts the three year clock on it. That is why catching up closes your exposure rather than just clearing the backlog.

Do I need a CPA for a Hawaii tax catch-up?

Not for this. Catching up on GET and TAT is state filing work, not income tax. We are a tax return preparer registered with the Hawaii Department of Taxation as a Verified Practitioner, which is what lets us file for you and deal with the Department. We are not a CPA firm and we do not touch income tax returns.

Can you file my Hawaii returns by the 20th?

Only once we have access to your accounts on Hawaii Tax Online, and getting that access takes a validation step that can fail. So we will not promise a specific deadline until access is confirmed. We will always tell you honestly where you stand.
