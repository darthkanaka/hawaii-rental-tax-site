Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

This policy explains what information Hawaii Rental Tax collects, why, who sees it, and how we protect it. We've tried to write it the way we'd explain it to you in person.

Hawaii Rental Tax is a trade name of Veex Photo LLC, a Hawaii limited liability company. When we say "we," "us," or "our," we mean Veex Photo LLC doing business as Hawaii Rental Tax. When we say "you," we mean anyone who visits our website, joins our email list, or becomes a client.

The short version

  • We collect what we need to file your Hawaii rental taxes, and nothing more.
  • We use your tax information only to prepare and file your returns. Hawaii law (HRS 231-15.5) makes it a crime for a tax return preparer to disclose or use it for anything else without your written consent, and we take that seriously.
  • We never sell your information, and we don't share it for advertising.
  • The only outside parties who see it are the Hawaii Department of Taxation, your county, and the few service providers we need to run the business, listed below.
  • You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to fix it, and ask us to delete what the law doesn't require us to keep.

1. Information we collect

If you visit our website

  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to see how people find and use the site: which pages get read, what gets clicked, how far people scroll, and roughly where visitors come from. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technology to do this. We have not turned on Google's advertising features, and we don't send Google your name or email address.
  • How you found us. The first time you visit, your browser stores a small note in its local storage with the page you landed on, the site that sent you (if any), and any campaign tags in the link (things like "utm_source"). This stays in your browser. It only reaches us if you sign up for our list.
  • Standard server records. Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records basic technical information such as IP addresses when pages are requested. We don't have access to those records beyond what GitHub provides to site owners.

If you join our email list

  • Your email address.
  • Which form you used, the page you were on, and the "how you found us" note described above.

If you email or call us

  • Whatever you choose to send us, plus your email address or phone number.

If you become a client

To prepare and file your returns, we collect:

  • Your name, mailing address, email, and phone number.
  • If you sign for a business or trust, its name and your authority to sign for it.
  • Your Social Security number or federal Employer Identification Number, as used on your Hawaii tax accounts.
  • Your Hawaii Tax ID, General Excise Tax license number, and Transient Accommodations Tax registration numbers, plus any county TAT account.
  • The address and details of each rental property you enroll.
  • Your rental income records: booking platform statements (Airbnb, VRBO, and similar), direct booking records, leases, and the other figures needed to compute your taxes.
  • Notices and letters you receive from the Department of Taxation or your county.
  • Your signed engagement letter and your signed Form N-848 (Hawaii Power of Attorney).
  • Your filing history with us: the returns we prepared, confirmation numbers, and our notes.

Payment information

Payments are processed by Stripe. When you pay, you give your card or bank details directly to Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe tells us your name, email, the last four digits and brand of your card, and whether a payment went through.

2. How we use it

  • Website and list information: to run and improve the site, to send you what you signed up for (the founding list email and the catch-up checklist), and to follow up about becoming a client. Every marketing email includes a way to unsubscribe.
  • Client information: to prepare, review, and file your Hawaii GET, state TAT, and county TAT returns; to act for you with the Department of Taxation and your county under your Form N-848; to remind you of deadlines; to answer notices; to bill you; and to keep the records we need to support the returns we filed.

We don't use client tax information for anything else. We don't use it to market other products, and we don't combine it with outside data to profile you.

3. Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it with anyone for advertising.

We share information only in these situations:

  1. The Hawaii Department of Taxation and your county, to file your returns and represent you under your Form N-848.
  2. Service providers who help us run the business. Each one gets only what it needs to do its job:
  3. Stripe (payment processing).
  4. Supabase (the database that stores our email list and client records).
  5. The secure file storage we use for client documents, which encrypts files in transit and at rest.
  6. Google (Google Analytics for the website, and Gmail for email).
  7. GitHub (website hosting).
  8. Namecheap (our domain and the forwarding of email sent to aloha@hawaiirentaltax.com).
  9. With your written consent or at your request. For example, if you ask us to send your filing records to your CPA or property manager.
  10. When the law requires it, such as a court order or a disclosure permitted under HRS 231-3.
  11. If the business changes hands. If Hawaii Rental Tax is ever sold or merged, we would ask for your written consent before transferring your client records to the new owner.

As a client, you give your written consent to these disclosures in your engagement letter.

4. How we protect it

We treat your tax information with the care you'd expect from anyone holding your Social Security number. That includes:

  • Administrative, technical, and physical safeguards under a written information security program, as the FTC Safeguards Rule expects of tax preparers.
  • Two-step login on every account that holds client information.
  • Encryption of your information while it moves between systems and while it's stored.
  • A secure way to send us your documents. Please don't email us your Social Security number or tax documents. When you become a client, we'll give you a secure way to send them.
  • Access limited to the people doing your work.
  • Service providers chosen for their security, and checked on.

No system is perfect, and we can't promise that information will never be exposed. We can promise to protect it carefully and to tell you promptly if something goes wrong.

5. If there's a breach

If your personal information is exposed in a way that could put you at risk, we'll notify you without unreasonable delay, as Hawaii law (HRS chapter 487N) requires. We'll tell you what happened, what kind of information was involved, what we've done about it, how to reach us, and what steps you can take to protect yourself. Where the law requires it, we'll also notify the Hawaii Office of Consumer Protection, the credit reporting agencies, and the Federal Trade Commission.

6. How long we keep it

  • Email list: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.
  • Client tax records (returns, workpapers, source records, N-848s, engagement letters): for seven years after the due date of the last return we filed for you, so we can support those returns if a period is ever questioned.
  • Billing records: as long as tax and accounting rules require.
  • Website analytics: kept in Google Analytics for no more than 14 months, then deleted by Google.

When we no longer need information, we destroy it so it can't be read or rebuilt: paper is shredded, and electronic files are permanently deleted, as HRS chapter 487R requires.

7. Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from our emails with the link at the bottom of any of them, or by emailing us. We'll honor it within 10 business days.
  • See, correct, or delete your information. Email aloha@hawaiirentaltax.com and tell us what you'd like. We'll respond within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we can't delete records the law requires us to keep.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout, or by blocking cookies in your browser.
  • Clear the "how you found us" note by clearing your browser's site data for hawaiirentaltax.com.

8. Do Not Track and cross-site tracking

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal. There's no agreed standard for what sites should do with it, so our site doesn't change its behavior when it receives one. Google Analytics may collect information about your activity on our site that Google can connect with activity on other sites that use Google services. You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at www.google.com/policies/privacy/partners/.

9. Privacy rights under state law

We don't sell or share personal information for advertising, and we don't use it to profile you. Because of our size, comprehensive state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act don't currently apply to us, but we offer the choices in Section 7 to everyone, wherever you live.

10. Children

Our services are for adults who own or manage rental property. Our website isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If we learn that we have, we'll delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change materially affects how we handle client information, we'll email clients before it takes effect.
