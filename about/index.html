Credentials

What you're getting, and what you're not

If you're about to give someone your Social Security number, you should know exactly who they are. So here it is without the varnish.

The business

Hawaii Rental Tax is a trade name of Veex Photo LLC, a Hawaii limited liability company in good standing. That's the name on our contracts and invoices. The trade name registration is still working its way through the state, which is why you'll see the LLC name where you might expect ours.

The registration that matters

I'm registered with the Hawaii Department of Taxation as a Verified Practitioner, ID T-043-817-7280. You'll see that number again: it goes on the Form N-848 you sign, which is the state's power of attorney. The registration is what lets me file your returns and deal with the Department on your behalf.

Being clear about what it isn't: it's a registration, not an endorsement. The Department doesn't vouch for us, recommend us, or have any opinion about us at all.

I also hold a current IRS preparer tax identification number, which federal law requires of anyone who prepares tax returns for money.

Insurance

We carry professional liability insurance, also called errors and omissions coverage, at $1,000,000 per claim. It's a claims-made policy in force now. Most people never ask, and the ones who do are usually property managers, who are right to.

What we're not

  • Not a CPA firm and not a law firm. Nobody here is an accountant or an attorney, and we don't pretend otherwise.
  • We don't do income tax. Not federal, not state. Your income tax stays with whoever does it now, and we'll hand them clean records of everything we filed.
  • We don't give legal advice or represent you in an audit or an appeal unless we've agreed to that separately in writing.
  • We don't manage your property or collect your rent. Collecting rent for someone else in Hawaii makes you a rent collector with its own filing duties, and we're deliberately not that.
  • We never hold your tax money. When we file, the payment goes from your own bank account to the state. It doesn't pass through us, so there's no account of ours for it to get stuck in.

Your information

How your information is protected

Preparing tax returns for money makes us a financial institution under federal law, which sounds absurd for a household business but means something real: we have to keep a written information security plan and actually follow it. We do. Here's what it amounts to in practice.

  • Two-step login on every system that touches client information, without exception.
  • Encrypted in transit and at rest, on the laptop as well as in the cloud.
  • A secure folder for your documents. Please don't email us your Social Security number, bank details or tax paperwork. We'll send you a link instead, and if something does arrive by email we move it and delete it.
  • Only the people doing your work can see it. There are two of us, and access is limited even between us.
  • Hawaii law makes it a crime for a tax return preparer to use or disclose your information for anything beyond your returns without your written consent. We treat that as the floor, not the ceiling.
  • We keep records for seven years after the last return we file for you, then destroy them properly.

The full detail is in our privacy policy, which is written to be read rather than skimmed past.
